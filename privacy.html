Who we are

Stickee Story is a trading name operated by Ian Griggs. I operate the website stickeestory.com. You can reach me at hello@stickeestory.com with any questions about this policy or your data.

What data we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form on this site:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name
  • The message you write to us

We do not collect this information automatically and we do not use it for any purpose other than responding to your enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or any other tracking technology. We do not profile visitors and we do not share browsing data with third parties.

How your data is stored

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, our hosting provider. Netlify's own privacy policy governs how they handle that data. We access your submission only to respond to you, and we do not retain it longer than necessary for that purpose.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to or restrict how we use it
  • Receive a copy of it in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@stickeestory.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes (for example, if we add analytics or a newsletter), we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically. Continued use of the site after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
