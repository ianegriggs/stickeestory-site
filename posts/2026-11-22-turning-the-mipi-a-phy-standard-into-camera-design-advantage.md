A technical whitepaper for Valens Semiconductor explaining why automotive camera makers are moving from discrete serializer chips to sensors with MIPI A-PHY built in, and the resulting gains in size, power, cost, and supply chain simplicity. Backed by on-record quotes from Sony and OmniVision executives and Sony’s IMX828 sensor launch as a real-world case in point. I wrote it to translate the engineering choices into business value for a technical audience.
