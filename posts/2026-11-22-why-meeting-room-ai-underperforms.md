A whitepaper for Valens Semiconductor explaining why AI features in Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Webex underperform in physical meeting rooms, and how a camera architecture borrowed from automotive sensor technology solves it. Written to translate a dense technical problem (bandwidth limits, compressed video, edge versus centralized processing) into a clear argument for a non-engineering audience, while positioning Valens’ chipset as the solution. Structured and written end to end, with supporting industry research to back the technical claims.
