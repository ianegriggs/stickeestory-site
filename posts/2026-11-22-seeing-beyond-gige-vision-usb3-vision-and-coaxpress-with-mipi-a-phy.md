A technical whitepaper for Valens Semiconductor explaining why legacy industrial machine vision standards, GigE Vision, USB3 Vision, and CoaXPress, each force tradeoffs in bandwidth, cable length, latency, and reliability, and how the VA7000 chipset, built on automotive-grade MIPI A-PHY, resolves them. Covers diagnostics, EMI resilience, multi-camera synchronization, and a full standards comparison table.
