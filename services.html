What is full-stack content marketing and narrative intelligence?+

Narrative intelligence (brand story audit and development), content strategy (channel and format mapping), and asset creation (the actual copy and assets). One engagement, from the first strategic insight to the final deliverable.

What is narrative intelligence?+

The process of finding the story worth telling and crystallizing it into a single ownable idea grounded in behavioral science. The result is a core narrative that holds up across every format, channel, and audience.

How is Stickee Story different from a strategy consultancy or content marketing firm?+

The same person who builds the strategy crafts the copy. No handoff to a junior team or separate production shop, and no framework without deliverables. The thinking and the doing happen in the same room, which means the original insight survives all the way to the final piece.

What content formats does Stickee Story write?+

Articles, email sequences, paid ads, video scripts, social content, landing pages, PR and press outreach, brand foundations, event materials, design briefs, and retail packaging. 12 core formats with 45+ specific variations, each one rooted in your concept.

Can Stickee Story optimize content for AI answer engines?+

Yes. Any deliverable can be shaped for human readers or structured for how AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews surface and cite content. The brief determines the format. Both are available.

What is Generative Engine Optimization and why does it matter?+

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content so AI-powered search engines can find, understand, and quote it accurately. As AI answer engines replace traditional search for many queries, content that is not structured for them becomes invisible. GEO is now a core part of any content strategy built to last.
